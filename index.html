REQUIRED BEFORE POSTING

Community guidelines

Please read these before participating in the discussion boards.

These boards are for students to help each other study. To keep them useful and safe for everyone:

Be respectful. Treat others how you'd want to be treated. No insults, harassment, or personal attacks.

Don't share assessment answers. Discussing concepts and study methods is encouraged. Sharing answers to current assessments, IAs, or exams is academic misconduct and will be removed.

Stay on topic. Each board is for its subject. Off-topic posts may be removed.

No personal information. Don't share your full name, address, phone number, school details, or anyone else's.

No inappropriate content. Nothing sexual, violent, or discriminatory. This is a study space.

Report problems. If you see a post that breaks these guidelines, use the Report button. Reports are reviewed by the admin.

Consequences. Posts that break these guidelines will be deleted. Repeated violations may result in posting access being revoked.

Privacy notice. Your display name and posts are visible to all users. The admin can see reported posts and your basic account activity (in aggregate).
